# Portfolio illustration prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These are original anonymous
scenario illustrations, not screenshots or client source material. Source PNGs
are retained here; the build exports responsive, metadata-free derivatives.

## Shared direction

Polished wide editorial illustration for a professional software engineering
portfolio. Landscape 3:2 composition, white background, grounded scene with
generous clean margins. Crisp fine indigo outlines, pale periwinkle and cool
neutral fills. Limited palette: #1a237e, #dfe4fa, #f5f6fb, #687086 and white.
Refined 2D drawing with architectural linework. Full objects visible and
recognizable at thumbnail size. No readable text, numbers, branding, logos,
watermarks, people, hands, decorative arrows, glow, clay or floating icon clouds.

## Scene prompts

- `b2b.png`: An account folder, a recognizable billing document and a small access
  key in one business-service workspace. Abstract company profile marks, record
  lines and square stamps; no actual interface or identifiable records.
- `commerce.png`: A finely drawn unbranded shoe beside a smartphone showing the
  same product from another angle. A neutral product card suggests the catalog.
  Show online product exploration on a phone, without branding or readable labels.
- `website.png`: A product publication shown as a broad page and a narrow mobile
  page. A modern desk lamp is the example product, with content lines and a video
  frame. Show the same editorial structure across page sizes.
- `editorial.png`: An article notebook and compact history chart, with three
  visually distinguishable series and a magnifying glass. No rank numbers,
  keywords or axis labels. An analysis workspace rather than financial trading.
- `services.png`: A portfolio contact sheet of handmade ceramic objects, an open
  appointment diary and conversation cards. The subject is a service
  professional's work and an ongoing client relationship.
- `assistant.png`: A compact desktop workstation showing abstract task and code
  lines, an open email envelope and a completed-task checklist. Grounded objects,
  no cables, server racks, robots, brains or readable code.
- `house.png`: An architectural cutaway of a modest contemporary two-storey home
  on a fictional sloping terrain slab. Visible wall thickness, floors, openings
  and stairs. A thin sun-path arc. Entire model visible; no real map, plot
  identifier, coordinates, people or labels.
