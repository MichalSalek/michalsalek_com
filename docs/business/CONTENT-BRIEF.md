# Offer and content brief

Atomic Concept helps owners of small and medium businesses commission software
that fits their work: AI-assisted automation, integrations and reporting, websites,
applications and custom systems. The audience should not need technical knowledge.

Present Atomic Concept as an implementation company responsible for a complete
engagement: discovery, design, engineering, integration, testing, deployment and
agreed maintenance. Use company voice. No founder biography, career timeline,
recruitment framing, personal remote-work narrative or GitHub links in marketing.
Legal company identification can remain in the footer. Do not invent staff size,
clients, certifications or delivery guarantees to create a corporate appearance.

## Voice and evidence

Keep the owner's original practical voice: understand the need before promising a
solution; avoid unnecessary complexity; make future maintenance understandable;
use technology as a tool. The approved copy direction uses ordinary language,
states the service scope clearly and explains responsibility for delivery. Avoid
superiority, metaphors, slogans and language that talks down to the reader.
Preserve the substance of the original descriptions while improving clarity.

Use concrete example tasks. Examples are possibilities, never invented client
projects. Do not publish private product identities, internal sources, client data,
unsupported savings, deadlines, testimonials or ranking/citation guarantees.

## Offer structure

- AI integrations: interpret documents, classify enquiries and search permitted
  company materials, with verification and scoped running costs.
- Workflow automation: repeated sequences, reminders and approvals following
  agreed rules; AI is optional.
- Existing-system integrations: exchange data between tools that stay in use,
  account for missing information, duplicate records and failures.
- Business websites: a clear public offer, content, contact, language versions and
  technical discovery foundations.
- Custom applications and systems: accounts, permissions, job records, portals,
  bookings and payments, with deployment and handover.
- Reports and dashboards: useful consolidated information, defined measures,
  sources, access and update frequency.

These scopes can be combined within one project. Keep two relevant service links
per article rather than repeating the complete catalogue at every page ending.

Keep the existing engineering articles as deeper background. Do not redirect their
URLs to unrelated new pages. Preserve separate audio services under `/sound/`.
The enquiry should start with the task, current tools and desired result. No instant
quotes or automatic commitments. English and Polish carry the same scope.

## Visual boundary

Reuse the original components and design described in `DESIGN.md`. Content work
must not introduce new card effects, decorative captions or badges. The homepage
introduction has an explicit owner-requested heading/body layout and emphasis;
keep it structured. The selected C + E system uses flat service scenes and
localized interface examples, generated as lightweight SVGs. Keep a light central
document against the indigo background. New directions need approval.

## Evidence boundary

See `MARKET-RESEARCH.md` for sources and limitations. Adoption figures are not search
volumes, proof of willingness to pay or a measurement of service-market saturation.
Validate offer priority against actual enquiries before choosing an industry niche.
