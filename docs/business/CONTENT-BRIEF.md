# Offer and content brief

Atomic Concept helps owners of small and medium businesses commission software
that fits their work: AI-assisted automation, integrations and reporting, websites,
applications and custom systems. The audience should not need technical knowledge.

Michał Sałek is the person responsible for discovery, engineering, deployment and
maintenance. Professional software experience dates from 2019. Present capabilities
plainly; do not turn a recruitment profile into a list of tools or imply a large team.

## Voice and evidence

Keep the owner's original practical voice: understand the need before promising a
solution; avoid unnecessary complexity; make future maintenance understandable;
use technology as a tool. Preserve these original English ideas where useful:
"We don't promise miracles at the beginning", "The best job done is the one that
accomplishes your needs without overcomplicated solutions", and "Technology is a
tool, not a toy". Improve grammar without replacing substance with slogans.

Use concrete example tasks. Examples are possibilities, never invented client
projects. Do not publish private product identities, internal sources, client data,
unsupported savings, deadlines, testimonials or ranking/citation guarantees.

## Offer structure

- AI and automation: repetitive document, enquiry and information tasks, with
  verification, explicit human decisions and proportionate running costs.
- Integrations and reporting: connect existing tools, reduce repeated data entry,
  reconcile errors and make business information available to the owner.
- Websites and custom applications: from a useful service website to a customer
  portal or internal system; assess existing tools before writing new software.

Keep the existing engineering articles as deeper background. Do not redirect their
URLs to unrelated new pages. Preserve separate audio services under `/sound/`.
The enquiry should start with the task, current tools and desired result. No instant
quotes or automatic commitments. English and Polish carry the same scope.

## Visual boundary

must not introduce new card effects, decorative captions, badges or a new layout.

## Evidence boundary
